To the woman I love

This little piece of my heart is yours.

I made something for you. A place you can return to whenever you need to feel how much you mean to me. And there's a song waiting for us just below. ♡

01 · No matter the miles

My heart keeps you close.

Even when I cannot reach for your hand, my heart reaches for you. I love you regardless of the distance, the uncertainty, or anything life puts in our way. Being apart can never make what I feel for you any smaller.

02 · All your days

In the light, and in the dark.

I want to celebrate your brightest days and stay beside you through your darkest ones. When the world feels heavy, I want to listen, comfort you, and remind you that you never have to pretend to be okay with me. I want to be there for all of you, not just the easy moments.

03 · A promise

I will keep choosing you.

You are the woman I love. Every single day, I want to protect your peace, care for your heart, respect your dreams, and be someone you can count on. I want to show you my love through patience, kindness, and the little things I do, not only through words.

04 · Your happiness

I want to see you so happy.

As long as I am part of your life, I will do my best, every day, to make you feel loved, safe, and truly, wonderfully happy. I cannot promise that every day will be perfect. But I can promise to keep bringing warmth, tenderness, and little reasons to smile into your world.

05 · Whenever you need me

Let these words feel like a hug.

If you ever miss me, close your eyes for a moment. Imagine my arms around you and my voice whispering: “I am here. I care about you. I love you.” Until I can hold you for real, I hope this little place reminds you that you are never far from my heart.

With all my love, always. ♡

Sending you the biggest, warmest hug. I love you. 💗
