To my Roby, with an open heart

My love,

Sometimes something small turns into a difficult conversation, and suddenly we feel far away from each other. I don’t want those moments to slowly wear down something so precious to me: us.

I know I can overexplain. When I’m afraid of being misunderstood, I repeat myself, even when what we need is a little more listening. I understand that this can be tiring, and I’m sorry for my part in that. I want to speak more simply, listen more carefully, and know when to pause.

I see that you’re trying too, Roby. I know we’re both learning. Real change doesn’t happen overnight, and I don’t expect either of us to be perfect.

There’s something I especially want you to know: my struggle is not against you. It’s against the misunderstanding, the tension, and the moments that make us forget we’re on the same side.

When things feel difficult, I hope we can choose kindness over coldness. And when one of us needs space, I want us to be able to say that gently, without fear, and find our way back to the conversation when we’re ready.

I don’t want a love that feels exhausting. I want us to feel safe enough to be honest, patient enough to understand, and gentle enough to protect what we’ve built.

I love you so much. I’m not promising that I’ll never make another mistake. I’m promising to keep learning and to show you, through my actions, that our relationship matters.
